# Participation

External code contributions are not currently being solicited pending publication of contribution and intellectual-property terms. No contributor license or CLA is established here.

Issue reports, discussion, independent adapter development and publication of findings are welcome. Please use the project's issue/discussion channels when available. Do not include credentials or private runtime data in reports.

Independent adapters may be published, distributed, licensed and sold under their authors' chosen terms, subject to the boundaries in [LICENSE](LICENSE). Truthful findings, including criticism and negative results, may be published. These permissions do not authorize redistribution of the Harness or substantial modified Harness code.
